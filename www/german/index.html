One learning loop

Memory first. Communication always.

01

Meet the word

See its sound, meaning, article, and forms together before you are asked to produce it.

02

Retrieve it

Type, hear, and recall it in both directions. Correct answers expand the next interval.

03

Use it

Complete a message, write a sentence, or speak. Productive knowledge gets its own practice.

04

Return on time

Weak and overdue words rise automatically. Strong words wait, keeping sessions short.
